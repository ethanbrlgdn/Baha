Baha!

A typhoon is coming and the water is rising. Carry your family, pets and important things up the stairs before the ground floor floods.

How to play

  • Move: arrow keys or WASD (on phones, the buttons below)
  • Grab / drop: Space or E (on phones, the Grab button)
  • Save it: carry it onto the stairs (UP). One thing at a time, and heavy things slow you down!
  • Pause: P or Esc
